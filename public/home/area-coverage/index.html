Which areas do you cover?

All 32 London boroughs, the City of London, and areas within the M25.

How do I check if my postcode is covered?

Enter your postcode in our instant quote form. If it’s within our coverage area, you’ll see your price straight away. Postcodes outside the M25 will show as not covered.

Does the price change depending on where I am?

Prices can vary slightly by zone. Central London bookings include a small supplement to cover congestion and parking costs, and some outer areas carry a small travel supplement. Any supplement is shown in your quote before you book.

Do you cover areas outside the M25?

Our online booking covers properties within the M25 only. For larger commercial or portfolio work just outside this area, get in touch and we’ll let you know if we can help.

Can you cover properties in several boroughs at once?

Yes. We regularly work with landlords and agents whose portfolios span multiple London boroughs, and can schedule visits to suit.
