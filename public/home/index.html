How quickly will I receive my certificate after the inspection?

Every certificate is issued within 24 hours of the inspection — usually the same evening. You receive it by email as an official PDF, ready to share with tenants or agents.

Are your engineers Gas Safe registered?

Yes. Every gas engineer we send is Gas Safe registered. Electrical work is carried out by NICEIC affiliated electricians.

How often does a landlord need a Gas Safety Certificate?

Landlords must have gas appliances and flues checked every 12 months. A new CP12 is required before a new tenancy starts if the current certificate has expired.

How often is an EICR required for rental properties?

Residential rental properties in England need an EICR at least every 5 years, or at the start of a new tenancy. HMOs and commercial properties may have different requirements.

Do I need an EPC for my rental property?

Yes. A valid EPC (minimum rating E) must be provided to tenants before marketing a property and is required for every new tenancy.

What happens if my property fails an inspection?

Your engineer will explain any issues found and advise on remedial work. We can often return to re-inspect once faults are fixed so you can obtain a compliant certificate.

Do you cover my area?

We cover all 32 London boroughs and the wider M25 corridor, including Essex, Kent, Surrey, Hertfordshire, and surrounding counties. WhatsApp us with your postcode to confirm.

Can I book multiple certificates for the same property?

Absolutely. You can book gas, electrical, fire, and EPC certificates in one visit where possible — saving time and reducing disruption for you and your tenants.
