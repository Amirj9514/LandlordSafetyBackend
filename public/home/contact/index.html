How do I book a service?

For residential certificates, use the Book Now button to get an instant quote and book online. For commercial and installation work, use Get a Quote and we’ll come back to you with a tailored quote.

How do I pay?

You pay by invoice once your booking has been confirmed.

Do your prices include VAT?

We’re not VAT registered, so no VAT is added. The price you’re quoted is the full price.

What information do you need when I book?

The property address and postcode, the property type and number of bedrooms, the services you need, and contact details for whoever will provide access.

Can you arrange access with my tenants or letting agent?

Yes. We can coordinate directly with your tenants or agent. Landlords should give tenants at least 24 hours’ written notice before a visit.

What should I do in a gas emergency?

Don’t wait to contact us. Call the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999 immediately. Once the property has been made safe, we can book a visit to find and fix the cause.
