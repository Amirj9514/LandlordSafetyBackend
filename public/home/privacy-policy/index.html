Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy explains how Landlord Safety Inspection Limited (“we”, “us”) collects and uses personal information when you use our website, request a quote, book an inspection or contact us.

1. Who we are

We are Landlord Safety Inspection Limited, company number 16414146, registered in England & Wales. Our registered office is Swaffield Rd, SW18 3AH. We are registered with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), registration number ZC045827, and are the data controller for the information described here.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact details — your name, email address and phone number when you send an enquiry, request a quote or book.
  • Property and booking details — the property address and postcode, property type, number of bedrooms, the services you need, preferred dates, and access arrangements (including tenant or agent contact details you give us).
  • Messages — anything you send us by form, email, phone or WhatsApp.
  • Invoices and payments — invoice and payment records for work we carry out. We do not store full card details on this website.
  • Inspection records — findings, photographs and certificates produced during an inspection.
  • Technical information — basic technical data such as browser type and IP address in server logs, used for security and to keep the site running. See our Cookie Policy.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide quotes, arrange and carry out inspections and issue certificates and reports.
  • To contact you about your booking, including confirmations, access and follow-up.
  • To issue invoices and take payment.
  • To answer your questions and handle complaints.
  • To meet our legal, accounting and regulatory obligations and keep appropriate records of the work we do.
  • To keep our website and systems secure and working properly.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for automated decision-making that has legal effects on you.

4. Our lawful bases

We rely on: contract (to give you a quote and carry out the work you request); legal obligation (for example, tax and accounting records); and legitimate interests (running and securing our business and responding to enquiries). Where we need your consent, we will ask for it and you can withdraw it at any time.

5. Who we share it with

We share information only where needed to provide our service, and only with:

  • the engineers, inspectors and assessors who carry out your booking;
  • service providers that help us run our business, such as website hosting, email, messaging, invoicing and payment providers, who act on our instructions;
  • tenants or letting agents you ask us to coordinate access with;
  • professional advisers, regulators or authorities where we are legally required to.

Certificates and reports are provided to you. We do not pass them to third parties unless you ask us to or the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes above. Enquiries that do not lead to a booking are normally deleted within 12 months. Booking, invoice and certificate records are kept for at least six years to meet accounting and legal requirements, and longer where the law or a dispute requires.

7. International transfers

Where a provider processes information outside the UK, we make sure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or an adequacy decision.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including access controls and encrypted connections. No online service is completely secure, so please take care when sending sensitive details.

9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to: be informed about how we use your data; access your data; have inaccurate data corrected; have data erased in certain cases; restrict or object to our processing; and receive your data in a portable format. To use any of these rights, contact us at sales@landlordsafetyinspection.co.uk. We will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
