Cookie Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy explains how our website uses cookies and similar technologies. Please read it with our Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. Similar technologies, such as browser local storage, work in a comparable way. They help websites work, remember your choices and understand how a site is used.

  • Session cookies are deleted when you close your browser.
  • Persistent cookies stay on your device until they expire or you delete them.
  • First-party cookies are set by the website you are visiting. Third-party cookies are set by other organisations, for example advertising or analytics providers.

2. What we use

Our website does not currently use advertising or analytics cookies, and we do not track you across other websites. We use only the following:

  • Strictly necessary: server and security. Our servers may set technical cookies or log basic request data needed to deliver pages, keep the site secure and prevent abuse.
  • Functional: browser local storage. Our instant estimate tool stores a copy of our service and price list in your browser’s local storage so estimates load quickly. It contains no personal information and stays on your device.

These are essential to how the site works, so they do not need your consent under the UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR).

3. Third-party content

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google to fetch them, and Google may receive your IP address as part of that request. Links to services such as WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Facebook and Companies House take you to websites run by other organisations, which have their own cookie policies.

4. If this changes

If we add analytics, advertising or other non-essential cookies in future, we will update this page and ask for your consent before setting them. You will be able to accept or reject them and change your choice at any time.

5. Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies and clear local storage at any time in your browser settings, usually under “Privacy” or “Site data”:

  • Chrome: Settings › Privacy and security › Third-party cookies / Delete browsing data
  • Safari: Settings › Safari › Advanced › Website Data (iPhone), or Settings › Privacy (Mac)
  • Firefox: Settings › Privacy & Security › Cookies and Site Data
  • Edge: Settings › Cookies and site permissions

Blocking or clearing these may make our estimate tool slightly slower to load. For more guidance, visit aboutcookies.org.uk or ico.org.uk.
