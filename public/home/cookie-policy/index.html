Cookie Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy explains how our website uses cookies and similar technologies. It should be read with our Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. Similar technologies, such as browser local storage, work in a comparable way. They are used to make websites work, remember your choices and understand how a site is used.

2. What we use

At the time of writing, our website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. We use only the following:

  • Strictly necessary — server and security. Our servers may set technical cookies or log basic request data that are needed to deliver pages, keep the site secure and prevent abuse.
  • Functional — browser local storage. Our instant estimate tool stores a copy of our service and price list in your browser’s local storage so the estimate loads quickly. It does not contain personal information and stays on your device.

These are essential to how the site works, so they do not need your consent under the UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR).

3. Third-party content

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google to fetch them, and Google may receive your IP address as part of that request. Links to services such as WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Facebook and Companies House take you to sites run by other organisations, which have their own cookie policies.

4. If this changes

If we add analytics, advertising or other non-essential cookies in future, we will update this page and ask for your consent first where the law requires it.

5. Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies and clear local storage at any time in your browser settings. Doing so may mean the estimate tool takes slightly longer to load. For guidance, visit aboutcookies.org or the ICO’s guidance on cookies.
