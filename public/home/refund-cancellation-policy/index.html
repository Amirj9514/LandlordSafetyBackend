Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy explains how to cancel or rearrange a booking with Landlord Safety Inspection Limited, and when you can expect a refund. It forms part of our Terms and Conditions.

Contents

    Contents

      1. Rearranging or cancelling a booking

      To rearrange or cancel, contact us at sales@landlordsafetyinspection.co.uk, call 020 4581 8191 or message us on WhatsApp, quoting your invoice reference (INV-Ref), name and property address. We will confirm any change in writing.

      Inspections and certificates

      • 24 hours’ notice or more, or any time the day before your appointment: free to cancel or rearrange.
      • On the day of your appointment:
        • If your appointment is in the afternoon and you tell us before 10am, you can cancel or rearrange free of charge, as long as our engineer is not already on the way.
        • Otherwise, a call-out charge applies: £30 for gas and EPC, £40 for all other inspections.

      Remedial, repair and installation work

      • Rearranging: free with at least 48 hours’ notice. With less than 24 hours’ notice, a £50 call-out charge applies.
      • Cancelling with 48 hours’ notice or more: your deposit is refunded in full if we have not yet bought materials for your job. If materials have already been bought, their cost is deducted and the materials are handed over to you.
      • Cancelling with less than 48 hours’ notice: the cost of any materials already bought plus a £50 call-out charge is deducted, and the rest of your deposit is refunded.

      2. Missed or failed visits

      If our engineer arrives at the agreed time and cannot carry out the work for a reason outside our control, a failed visit fee applies per engineer: £30 for gas and EPC, £40 for all other inspections and £75 for remedial work. Reasons include:

      • no access to the property;
      • a wrong address;
      • no gas or electricity supply;
      • no access to the meter or appliances;
      • no adult present at the property.

      Any congestion charge or parking costs paid by the engineer are also charged, as set out in our Terms and Conditions. A new appointment can be arranged, and any payment already made can be used toward it, less these charges.

      3. Your 14-day right to cancel (consumers)

      If you are an individual buying for yourself and you booked online or by phone, you have the right to cancel within 14 days of the booking being confirmed, without giving a reason, under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013.

      • If you ask us to carry out the service within that 14-day period, you must pay for the work done up to the point you cancel, in proportion to the full price.
      • Once the visit has been carried out, the service is complete and cannot be cancelled or refunded for any reason, including if you no longer need it.
      • This right does not apply to business customers, such as commercial landlords or companies booking for business purposes.

      4. Priority Service (£99)

      The £99 Priority Service fee covers rescheduling work already assigned to our engineer, or arranging an engineer specifically for your job, so it takes priority. It is not refundable once the priority appointment has been confirmed, unless we fail to attend or issue the certificate as agreed.

      5. When we cancel

      If we have to cancel or rearrange, for example because of illness, severe weather or an engineer being unavailable, we will let you know as soon as possible and offer a new date.

      • Inspections: if you prefer not to rebook, we will refund the full amount paid for that booking.
      • Remedial work: materials already bought for your job will be kept for the new date. If you prefer not to rebook, we will refund the rest of your deposit and hand the materials over to you.

      6. What an inspection includes

      • An inspection is a test of the installation or appliances only. It does not include repairs, replacement parts or fixing faults.
      • Anything found during the inspection that needs work will be charged separately, including labour. For example, if a gas safety inspection finds no carbon monoxide (CO) alarm and you ask us to fit one, our engineer can supply and install one for £35.
      • No refund if the inspection fails. If your property fails an inspection, the inspection has still been carried out and the fee is not refundable. Our engineer must follow safety regulations, and our team or engineer will tell you what work is needed and quote for it.
      • Re-inspection after a failed certificate: if a certificate fails because of a hazard or a legal requirement and you choose not to have the remedial work done by us, we will need to revisit the property before we can issue a pass certificate. The standard inspection charge applies to this revisit. Once the property passes, we will issue a valid certificate.

      If a certificate or report contains an error on our part, tell us within 14 days of receiving it and we will correct it at no extra cost.

      This does not affect your statutory rights.

      7. How refunds are paid

      Approved refunds are made to the original payment method, or by bank transfer for invoice payments. Before processing, we must complete the necessary checks, so please allow 3–14 working days after approval for the refund to reach you. Refunds under your 14-day right to cancel (section 3) are made within 14 days, as required by law.
