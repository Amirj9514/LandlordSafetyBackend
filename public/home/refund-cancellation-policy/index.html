Refund & Cancellation

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy explains how to cancel or rearrange a booking with Landlord Safety Inspection Limited, and when you can expect a refund. It forms part of our Terms & Conditions.

1. Rearranging or cancelling a booking

To rearrange or cancel, contact us at sales@landlordsafetyinspection.co.uk, call 020 4581 8191 or message us on WhatsApp, quoting your name and property address. We will confirm any change in writing.

  • 24 hours’ notice or more — you can rearrange or cancel free of charge.
  • Less than 24 hours’ notice — we may charge a reasonable fee that reflects the time and costs we have already incurred.

2. Missed or failed visits

If our engineer arrives at the agreed time and cannot get access, or it is unsafe to carry out the work, we may charge a reasonable fee for the wasted visit. A new appointment can be arranged, and your original payment may be used toward it, less that fee.

3. Your 14-day right to cancel (consumers)

If you are an individual buying for yourself and you booked online or by phone, you have the right to cancel within 14 days of the booking being confirmed, without giving a reason, under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013.

  • If you ask us to carry out the service within that 14-day period, you must pay for the work done up to the point you cancel, in proportion to the full price.
  • Once the service has been fully carried out, you lose the right to cancel.
  • This right does not apply to business customers, such as commercial landlords or companies booking for business purposes.

4. Next-day service

The additional next-day fee covers priority scheduling. It is not refundable once the next-day appointment has been confirmed, unless we fail to attend or issue the certificate as agreed.

5. When we cancel

If we have to cancel or rearrange, for example because of illness, severe weather or an engineer being unavailable, we will let you know as soon as possible and offer a new date or a full refund of any amount paid for that booking.

6. Problems with the service

If a certificate or report contains an error, or you are not satisfied with the work, tell us within 14 days of receiving it and we will correct it or put it right at no extra cost. If we cannot do so, we will refund the part of the price relating to the problem. If a certificate cannot be issued because the inspection found faults or hazards at the property, the inspection has still been carried out, so the inspection fee is not refundable. We will explain what is needed for a re-inspection.

This does not affect your statutory rights.

7. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method, or by bank transfer for invoice payments, within 14 days of us agreeing the refund.
