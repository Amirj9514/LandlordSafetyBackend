Our Story

It started in 2021, when we began working with a group of gas and electrical engineers. We kept seeing the same thing: property owners needed compliance certificates to get a home ready to rent or sell, or to keep their contracts in place.

We were providing gas and electrical services to customers across North London, serving hundreds of clients every month. But the problem was always the same. To stay compliant, customers had to find and chase several different contractors, each with their own availability, pricing and paperwork.

So we brought them together. Today, Landlord Safety Inspection (LLSI) gives landlords, property managers and estate agents one place for the compliance services they need most: gas, electrical, fire and energy. The engineers we work with are fully accredited, qualified and experienced, and we’ve been in this industry since 2021.

We arrange visits as early as the next day, and certificates are issued as early as 24 hours after the job is completed. Commercial certificates are typically issued within 2–4 working days.
