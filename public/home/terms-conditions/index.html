      6. Inspection results and certificates

      Certificates and reports record the condition of the property at the time of inspection. If an inspection finds a fault or hazard, we may be unable to issue a satisfactory certificate until it is put right. We will explain the findings and recommended next steps.

      If a gas or electrical installation is found to be dangerous, our engineer will follow industry safety procedures, which may include making it safe or disconnecting it, and will explain why.

      An inspection covers testing only. It does not include repairs or replacement parts, which are quoted separately. The inspection fee is not refundable if the property fails. If you have the required work done by someone else, a revisit is needed before we can issue a pass certificate, and the standard inspection charge applies. See our Refund and Cancellation Policy for details.

      Residential certificates are usually issued within 24 hours of the visit, and commercial certificates within 2–4 working days. These are estimates, not guarantees, and some services, such as fire risk assessments, can take longer.

      Keeping the property safe and meeting your legal obligations as a landlord or responsible person remains your responsibility. Our reports do not remove those duties.
