What certificates do landlords legally need for a rental property?

At minimum, a Gas Safety Certificate (if the property has gas appliances), an EICR, and a valid EPC with a minimum E rating. HMOs and multi-occupancy properties also require a Fire Risk Assessment and, in most cases, a certified fire alarm system.

Can I book multiple services in one visit?

Yes — many landlords bundle gas, electrical, fire and EPC certificates into a single visit to save time and minimise disruption to tenants.

Do all these services apply to every property, or only some?

It depends on the property. A Gas Safety Certificate only applies if the property has gas appliances, and Fire Risk Assessments or fire alarm systems mainly apply to HMOs and multi-occupancy buildings — while an EICR and EPC are required for virtually every rental property.

How often does each certificate need renewing?

Gas Safety Certificates are annual, an EICR is valid for up to 5 years, an EPC lasts 10 years, and Fire Risk Assessments should be reviewed at least annually or whenever the property changes.

What happens if I let a property without the required certificates?

You risk civil penalties of up to £30,000 per breach and invalidated insurance. A missing or expired certificate can also block a valid Section 21 or Section 8 notice, so you can’t legally regain possession of the property without one in place.

Do you offer installation as well as certification?

Yes. Alongside certification we install and repair consumer units, boilers, fire alarms, emergency lighting and CO/smoke alarms, all carried out by accredited engineers.

Are your engineers accredited for every service you offer?

Yes. Every engineer we send is Gas Safe registered and/or NICEIC affiliated depending on the service, so every certificate we issue is fully accredited and legally valid.

Do you cover HMOs as well as single-let properties?

Yes. We work with single-let properties, HMOs and multi-occupancy blocks, and can advise on the additional certificates HMOs typically require, such as Fire Risk Assessments and fire alarm systems.
