Requirements You Should Know

Gas work and diagnostics carried out by Gas Safe registered engineers, fully certified on completion.

  1. 01

    Who Can Carry It Out

    Only a Gas Safe registered engineer can legally install, alter, repair or certify gas work of any kind.

  2. 02

    Tightness Testing Required

    Any new or altered gas pipework must pass a tightness test before the system is put back into use.

  3. 03

    Certification Provided

    You'll receive a written record of the work and test results. For landlords, we can also issue a CP12 on the same visit.

  4. 04

    Common Fault Indicators

    If you smell gas, call the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999 straight away, open windows and don't use switches or flames. For boiler fault codes, pilot light problems or unusual flue noises, book a diagnostic visit.

  5. 05

    Duration of Your Appointment

    Typically 1–2 hours for a diagnostic visit or hob installation; more involved pipework relocation may take longer depending on access.

I can smell gas. What should I do?

Call the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999 immediately. Turn off the gas at the meter if it's safe to do so, open doors and windows, and don't use naked flames or operate electrical switches, on or off. If the smell is strong, leave the property. If anyone feels unwell, call 999. Once the emergency service has made it safe, book a diagnostic visit with us to find and fix the cause.

Can you move my gas hob to a different part of the kitchen?

Yes. We can extend or reroute gas pipework to reposition a hob, subject to a site assessment confirming the new position meets safety clearances, ventilation requirements and the manufacturer's instructions.

Do I need a Gas Safe engineer for a boiler repair?

Yes. By law, any work on gas appliances, including diagnosis and repair, must be carried out by a Gas Safe registered engineer. Always check the engineer's Gas Safe ID card, which shows the types of gas work they're qualified to do.

What happens if you find a gas leak or unsafe appliance during a job?

We follow the industry Unsafe Situations Procedure. If an installation is immediately dangerous, it will be isolated with your permission, labelled and recorded in writing. We'll then advise on the repair needed before it can be safely used again.

Will I get a certificate for the work?

Yes. New gas appliance installations are notified to Building Control through Gas Safe Register, and you'll receive a Building Regulations Compliance Certificate. Pipework alterations are tightness-tested and the results recorded.

Do you cover gas work outside London?

We cover all 32 London boroughs and within M25 London.
