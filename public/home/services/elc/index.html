Domestic Fire Safety Risk Assessment (FRA)

Emergency lighting is required in most commercial premises, HMOs and multi-occupancy buildings under the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005, to ensure occupants can safely evacuate if mains power fails. Testing and certification to BS 5266 confirms the system is working correctly and will last for its full rated duration — typically 3 hours — during an emergency.

A monthly function test and a full annual duration test are both required. Missing or failed testing can leave escape routes unlit during a power failure, and puts the responsible person at risk of enforcement action, unlimited fines, and — in serious cases — prosecution.

Landlord Safety Inspection tests and certifies emergency lighting systems to BS 5266, with engineers covering all 32 London boroughs and within M25 London. Your certificate is ready for HMO licensing, fire risk assessment records, or your own compliance file.

How often does emergency lighting need to be tested?

BS 5266 requires a short monthly function test and a full annual duration test that checks every fitting operates for its full rated duration — typically 3 hours — if mains power fails. We carry out the annual test and issue your certificate.

Is an Emergency Light Certificate a legal requirement?

Yes, for most commercial premises, HMOs and multi-occupancy buildings, under the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005. The responsible person must keep escape routes lit and be able to show evidence of regular testing.

What happens if a light fails the test?

Any faulty or non-compliant fitting is flagged on site, with the fault and its location noted on your report. Once the fitting is repaired or replaced, we can carry out a quick re-test to bring the certificate up to date.

Do all rental properties need emergency lighting?

Not every single let — it’s mandatory for HMOs, blocks of flats with communal escape routes, and other multi-occupancy or commercial buildings. If you’re unsure whether your property needs it, message us your address and we’ll confirm.

How long does the inspection take?

Typically 30–90 minutes, depending on the number of fittings and the size of the building. Larger blocks or bulk inspections can be scheduled around your access times — just message us on WhatsApp.

How quickly will I receive my certificate after the inspection?

Your official Emergency Light Certificate is issued within 1–2 days of the inspection, sent by email as a PDF that’s ready for HMO licensing, fire risk assessment records, or your own compliance file.
