Requirements You Should Know

What you need to know about alarm installation.

  1. 01

    Category Depends on Property Type

    Domestic and HMO systems are categorised (LD1, LD2, LD3) under BS 5839-6; commercial panel systems follow BS 5839-1 categories (L/P).

  2. 02

    Who Can Carry It Out

    Installation should be carried out by a competent, qualified engineer to ensure the system meets the required British Standard.

  3. 03

    Certification Provided

    You'll receive a compliance certificate confirming the system meets current regulations, plus a logbook for ongoing testing records.

  4. 04

    Testing After Installation

    Domestic alarms should be tested regularly by occupants. Panel systems need weekly tests and professional servicing at least every six months, to BS 5839-1.

  5. 05

    Duration of Your Appointment

    A simple alarm kit is usually completed within a few hours; full panel installations for larger buildings may take a full day or more.

Do I need a panel system, or are interlinked alarms enough?

Most commercial premises need a panel-based fire alarm system designed to BS 5839-1. Interlinked domestic alarms are generally only suitable for homes and smaller HMOs, while larger HMOs and mixed-use buildings often need a panel system too. Your Fire Risk Assessment will confirm what's required, and we'll advise after a site assessment.

What's the difference between a conventional and an addressable system?

A conventional system divides the building into zones, so the panel shows which area an alarm came from. An addressable system identifies the exact device that triggered, making it quicker to locate a fire or fault. Addressable systems are usually better suited to larger or multi-floor buildings.

How often does the system need testing once installed?

BS 5839-1 recommends a weekly test by the responsible person, operating a different manual call point each time, plus a professional inspection and service roughly every 6 months. We can set up a scheduled maintenance plan for you.

What certificates will I receive?

On completion, you'll receive the design, installation and commissioning certificates for the system under BS 5839-1, along with a logbook for recording tests, faults and servicing.

Can you install across multiple sites or a whole building in one project?

Yes. We handle everything from single units through to multi-floor buildings and multi-site portfolios, planned around your operations to minimise disruption.
