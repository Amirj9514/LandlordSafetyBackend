Requirements You Should Know

The fire safety rules you need to stay on top of — clearly explained.

  1. 01

    Legal Basis

    The Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005 places a duty on the responsible person to provide adequate fire safety equipment, signage and emergency lighting.

  2. 02

    Standards Followed

    Emergency lighting follows BS 5266, extinguishers BS 5306 and safety signs BS ISO 7010.

  3. 03

    Ongoing Testing & Servicing

    Emergency lighting needs monthly and annual testing; extinguishers require an annual service by a competent person — we can include this in scheduled visits.

  4. 04

    Certification Provided

    An installation record is issued confirming what's fitted and where, useful for your fire risk assessment and insurer's records.

  5. 05

    Duration of Your Appointment

    Typically 1–3 hours for a standard property; larger or multi-floor commercial premises may take longer depending on scope.

Do I need a Fire Risk Assessment before installing this equipment?

For commercial premises and the common areas of residential buildings, a Fire Risk Assessment is a legal requirement under the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005, and it should determine what equipment is needed and where. If you don't have one, we can carry one out, or advise on equipment after a site visit.

What type of fire extinguisher do I need?

It depends on the fire risks present: ordinary combustibles like paper and wood, flammable liquids, electrical equipment, or cooking oils in kitchens. Each needs a different extinguisher type, such as water, foam, CO2, powder or wet chemical. We confirm the right types, number and positions during the site assessment, in line with BS 5306-8.

How often do fire extinguishers need servicing?

BS 5306-3 recommends a basic service by a competent person every year, with extended servicing or replacement at longer intervals depending on the extinguisher type. Between services, the responsible person should carry out regular visual checks.

Can you service existing fire extinguishers as well as install new ones?

Yes. We can service and tag existing extinguishers during the same visit, and replace any that are damaged, out of date or unsuitable for the risk.

How often does fire signage need replacing?

Signage doesn't expire. Under the Health and Safety (Safety Signs and Signals) Regulations 1996, though, it must stay clearly visible, legible and correctly positioned. It should be checked regularly and updated if it's damaged, faded, or the building layout changes. We install signage using the standard BS ISO 7010 symbols.
