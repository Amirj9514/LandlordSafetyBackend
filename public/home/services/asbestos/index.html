Asbestos Survey

If your property was built before 2000, it may contain asbestos-containing materials, commonly found in insulation, textured coatings, floor tiles, and pipe lagging. A survey identifies where these materials are, their condition, and whether they need managing or removing before any renovation, refurbishment or demolition work begins.

Under the Control of Asbestos Regulations 2012, the "duty holder" — typically the landlord or managing agent — is legally responsible for identifying and managing asbestos risk in the common areas of multi-occupancy residential buildings. Failure to do so can result in enforcement action and unlimited fines. For a single self-contained let, a survey isn't a standing legal duty, but is strongly recommended before any building work.

Requirements You Should Know

The fire safety rules landlords need to stay on top of — clearly explained.

  1. 01

    Who Needs One

    Required before renovation, refurbishment or demolition work on any pre-2000 property, and a standing legal duty for common areas of multi-occupancy residential buildings.

  2. 02

    The Duty Holder

    Under the Control of Asbestos Regulations 2012, the landlord or managing agent is responsible for identifying and managing asbestos risk in common areas.

  3. 03

    Survey Types

    A management survey identifies asbestos under normal occupied conditions; a refurbishment/demolition survey is more intrusive and required before construction work begins.

  4. 04

    Non-Compliance Penalties

    Failure to identify or manage known asbestos risk can result in unlimited fines and enforcement action against the duty holder.

  5. 05

    Duration of Your Appointment

    Typically 1–3 hours, depending on the size of the property and number of areas surveyed.

Do I need an asbestos survey for my property?

Properties built before 2000 may contain asbestos. The common areas of HMOs and blocks of flats fall under the legal duty to manage asbestos, and a survey is also needed before any refurbishment, renovation or demolition work.

What's the difference between a management survey and a refurbishment survey?

A management survey locates, as far as reasonably practicable, any asbestos that could be disturbed during normal use and routine maintenance. A refurbishment or demolition survey is more intrusive and is required before building work begins, covering the areas affected.

Who is legally responsible for managing asbestos?

Under Regulation 4 of the Control of Asbestos Regulations 2012, the dutyholder is whoever is responsible for maintenance or repair of the common areas, usually the landlord, freeholder or managing agent.

What happens if asbestos is found?

You'll receive a clear report on its location and condition, with recommendations on whether it can be safely managed in place or should be removed. Higher-risk removal work must be carried out by an HSE-licensed contractor.

Do single residential lettings need an asbestos survey?

The duty to manage doesn't apply inside a single private dwelling. A refurbishment survey is still needed before building work that could disturb asbestos.

We cover all 32 London boroughs and within M25 London.
