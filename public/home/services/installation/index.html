Diagnostics Service

When something's not working right — a tripping circuit, a boiler fault, an alarm going off for no clear reason — our engineers carry out a full diagnostic check to find the root cause, not just treat the symptom. We cover electrical faults, gas appliance issues, and fire alarm system faults, using the same accredited engineers who carry out your certification work.

A diagnostic visit is different from a certificate inspection: it's called out specifically to investigate a known issue, rather than a scheduled compliance check. Once the fault is identified, you'll get a clear explanation of what's wrong and a quote for any repair work, before anything further is carried out.

Requirements You Should Know

The fire safety rules landlords need to stay on top of — clearly explained.

  1. 01

    Call-Out vs Certification

    A diagnostic visit investigates a specific known issue, separate from a scheduled gas, electrical or fire safety inspection.

  2. 02

    Who Carries It Out

    Matched to the fault type — Gas Safe registered engineers for gas issues, NICEIC affiliated engineers for electrical faults.

  3. 03

    Repairs Are Quoted Separately

    The call-out covers diagnosis only; any repair work is quoted and agreed before it goes ahead.

  4. 04

    Landlord's Duty of Care

    Investigating and resolving reported faults promptly is part of a landlord's general duty of care to tenants.

  5. 05

    Duration of Your Appointment

    Typically 30–90 minutes, depending on the complexity of the fault.

How quickly will I receive my certificate after the inspection?

Every certificate is issued within 24 hours of the inspection — usually the same evening. You receive it by email as an official PDF, ready to share with tenants or agents.

Are your engineers Gas Safe registered?

Yes. Every gas engineer we send is Gas Safe registered. Electrical work is carried out by NICEIC affiliated electricians.

How often does a landlord need a Gas Safety Certificate?

Every 12 months. Landlords must have all gas appliances, fittings and flues checked by a Gas Safe registered engineer each year, give existing tenants a copy within 28 days of the check, give new tenants a copy before they move in, and keep each record for at least 2 years.

How often is an EICR required for rental properties?

At least every 5 years, or sooner if the previous report says so. Landlords must give tenants a copy within 28 days of the inspection, new tenants before they move in, and complete any required remedial work within 28 days (or sooner if the report specifies).

Do I need an EPC for my rental property?

Yes. A rental property must have a valid EPC rated E or above (unless a valid exemption is registered), and it must be shown to prospective tenants before they rent. An EPC stays valid for 10 years.

What happens if my property fails an inspection?

Your engineer will explain any issues found and advise on the remedial work needed — for an EICR this must be completed within 28 days. We can often carry out the repairs and re-inspect so you can obtain a compliant certificate.

Do you cover my area?

We cover all 32 London boroughs and the wider M25 corridor, including Essex, Kent, Surrey, Hertfordshire, and surrounding counties. WhatsApp us with your postcode to confirm.

Can I book multiple certificates for the same property?

Absolutely. You can book gas, electrical, fire, and EPC certificates in one visit where possible — saving time and reducing disruption for you and your tenants.
