Boiler Installation

Your boiler is the heart of your property's heating and hot water — and choosing the right type and getting it installed correctly affects everything from your energy bills to how quickly your system develops faults. Whether you're replacing an old, inefficient boiler or fitting one for the first time, the installation must be carried out by a Gas Safe registered engineer and notified under Building Regulations before it's signed off. We install and commission boilers from the manufacturers most commonly specified across London properties — including Worcester Bosch, Baxi, Vaillant, Ideal, Viessmann and Glow-worm — matched to your property's size, layout and hot water demand, with the manufacturer's warranty protected through correct commissioning.

Combi Boiler

Heats water on demand with no separate cylinder or tank — the most common choice for flats and smaller properties where space is limited.

System Boiler

Works with a hot water cylinder to store heated water, suited to properties with higher hot water demand or multiple bathrooms.

Regular (Conventional) Boiler

Uses both a hot water cylinder and a cold water feed tank — typically found in older properties, and usually replaced like-for-like rather than converted to another type.

Like-for-Like Swap

Same boiler type, same location — the quickest and most straightforward installation, with no extra pipework or relocation involved.

Requirements You Should Know

What you need to know before installing a new boiler.

  1. 01

    Building Regulations Notification

    Boiler installation is notifiable work — it must be self-certified by a Gas Safe registered installer or notified to Building Control.

  2. 02

    Who Can Carry It Out

    Only a Gas Safe registered engineer can legally install, commission or certify gas boiler work.

  3. 03

    Benchmark Certificate Required for Warranty

    Manufacturers require a Benchmark commissioning certificate for the warranty to be valid. We issue this on completion and register your boiler with the manufacturer, so your warranty is active from day one.

  4. 04

    Boiler Plus Standards

    In England, new boiler installations must include time and temperature controls, and new combis need at least one extra energy-saving measure, such as a smart thermostat or flue gas heat recovery.

  5. 05

    Duration of Your Appointment

    A straightforward like-for-like swap is usually completed in a day; relocations or boiler-type changes may take longer depending on pipework needed.

Do I need permission to install a new boiler?

No permission is needed, but by law all gas work must be carried out by a Gas Safe registered engineer. The installation is notified to Building Control through Gas Safe Register, and you'll receive a Building Regulations Compliance Certificate, all handled for you.

Will my new boiler come with a warranty?

Yes. Manufacturer warranties typically require the Benchmark commissioning checklist to be completed, which we do on completion, plus registration of the boiler with the manufacturer and an annual service. Warranty length and terms vary by manufacturer and model.

How long does a boiler installation take?

A straightforward like-for-like swap is usually completed in a day. More complex installations, such as relocating the boiler, converting the system type or upgrading pipework, may take longer.

Do I need a new boiler or just a service?

If your boiler is old, inefficient or repeatedly breaking down, replacement may be more cost-effective than ongoing repairs. We can advise after a site visit.

What do Building Regulations require with a new boiler?

In England, a new gas boiler must meet minimum efficiency standards and be fitted with suitable controls, such as a timer and room thermostat. The heating system should also be cleaned and treated with inhibitor to protect the new boiler. We cover all of this as part of the installation.

Do you cover boiler installation outside London?

We cover all 32 London boroughs and within M25 London.
