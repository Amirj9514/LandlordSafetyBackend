Domestic Gas Safety Certificate (CP12)

A Gas Safety Certificate, commonly known as a CP12, is a legal requirement for any rental property with gas appliances. It confirms that every gas appliance, fitting, pipework and flue you provide as a landlord — boilers, hobs, gas fires, cookers — has been checked and is safe to use. The inspection must be carried out at least once every 12 months by a Gas Safe registered engineer.

Missing or letting a certificate lapse is a criminal offence, not just an administrative one. It can lead to unlimited fines and, in serious cases, prosecution — and an expired or missing certificate also invalidates a Section 21 notice, meaning you can't regain possession of the property without a valid record on file. Requirements You Should Know

Our certified Gas Safety assessments are affordable and tailored for residential properties:

  1. 01

    Annual Renewal Window

    You can carry out the annual check-up up to 2 months early and keep the same deadline date, if you follow the rules. The 12-month interval runs from the date of inspection — not the date the certificate was issued, not the tenancy start, not when the certificate was handed to the tenant.

  2. 02

    What’s Covered

    A Gas Safe registered engineer checks each appliance for safe operation, correct ventilation, tightness of pipework, and adequate flue and chimney function to safely remove combustion products.

  3. 03

    Giving Tenants a Copy

    A copy of the current certificate must go to tenants within 28 days of the check, and to new tenants before they move in. Records must be kept for at least 2 years.

  4. 04

    Non Compliance

    Penalty for failure: unlimited fine, up to 6 months in prison, and an invalidated Section 21. Buildings insurance can also be voided by an expired or missing CP12.

  5. 05

    Duration of the Appointment

    A typical single-appliance check takes 20–30 minutes; a property with a boiler, hob and gas fire usually takes 45–60 minutes.

How often do I need a Gas Safety Certificate?

Every 12 months, without exception. You can carry out the check up to 2 months before the current certificate expires without losing your renewal date.

What happens if I don’t have a valid CP12?

It’s a criminal offence, not just an administrative one — it can lead to unlimited fines and, in serious cases, prosecution. A missing or expired certificate also invalidates a Section 21 notice, so you can’t regain possession of the property without a valid record in place.

What does the inspection actually check?

Every gas appliance, pipe, fitting and flue you provide as landlord — boilers, hobs, gas fires, cookers — checked for leaks, safe combustion, and correct ventilation.

Do I need to give tenants a copy?

Yes. A copy must go to tenants within 28 days of the check, and to new tenants before they move in. You’re required to keep records for at least 2 years.

What if an appliance fails the inspection?

The appliance will be marked as “At Risk” or “Immediately Dangerous” on the certificate and must not be used until repaired by a Gas Safe registered engineer. Your engineer will explain the fault and what remedial work is needed. We can re-inspect once repairs are complete so you can obtain a compliant CP12.
