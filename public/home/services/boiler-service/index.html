Boiler Service (Servicing)

An annual boiler service is routine maintenance carried out by a Gas Safe registered engineer — cleaning internal components, checking gas pressure and flow, testing safety devices, and confirming the boiler is running safely and efficiently. It's not the same as a Gas Safety Certificate (CP12): the CP12 covers gas safety across all appliances in the property, while a boiler service is maintenance specifically on the boiler itself, though the two are often booked together.

Servicing isn't a legal requirement for homeowners, but it's effectively standard practice for landlords as part of keeping the property's heating system safe and reliable, and most boiler manufacturers require annual servicing to keep the warranty valid.

Requirements You Should Know

The fire safety rules landlords need to stay on top of — clearly explained.

  1. 01

    Who Can Service a Boiler

    Only a Gas Safe registered engineer can legally service a gas boiler.

  2. 02

    Not a Legal Requirement — But Expected

    Servicing isn't mandated by law, but is standard landlord practice and often required by manufacturers to keep the warranty valid.

  3. 03

    Service vs CP12

    A boiler service is maintenance on the boiler itself; a CP12 covers gas safety across every appliance in the property. The two are separate but often booked together.

  4. 04

    Repairs Are Separate

    Servicing covers cleaning and safety checks only — any faults found and repairs needed are quoted separately.

  5. 05

    Duration of Your Appointment

    Typically 30–60 minutes for a standard combi boiler.
