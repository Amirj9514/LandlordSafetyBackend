Annual Boiler Service

An annual boiler service is routine maintenance carried out by a Gas Safe registered engineer — cleaning internal components, checking gas pressure and flow, testing safety devices, and confirming the boiler is running safely and efficiently. It's not the same as a Gas Safety Certificate (CP12): the CP12 covers gas safety across all appliances in the property, while a boiler service is maintenance specifically on the boiler itself, though the two are often booked together.

Servicing isn't a legal requirement for homeowners, but it's effectively standard practice for landlords as part of keeping the property's heating system safe and reliable, and most boiler manufacturers require annual servicing to keep the warranty valid.

Requirements You Should Know

What landlords need to know about boiler servicing.

  1. 01

    Who Can Service a Boiler

    Only a Gas Safe registered engineer can legally service a gas boiler.

  2. 02

    Not a Legal Requirement — But Expected

    There's no fixed legal servicing interval, but landlords must keep gas appliances maintained in a safe condition, and annual servicing is the standard way to show it. Most manufacturers also require it to keep the warranty valid.

  3. 03

    Service vs CP12

    A boiler service is maintenance on the boiler itself; a CP12 covers gas safety across every appliance in the property. The two are separate but often booked together.

  4. 04

    Repairs Are Separate

    Servicing covers cleaning and safety checks only — any faults found and repairs needed are quoted separately.

  5. 05

    Duration of Your Appointment

    Typically 30–60 minutes for a standard combi boiler.

Is an annual boiler service a legal requirement?

Not for homeowners or landlords. Landlords must have an annual gas safety check, which is different from a service. However, most boiler manufacturers require an annual service to keep the warranty valid, and regular servicing keeps the boiler running safely and efficiently.

What’s the difference between a boiler service and a Gas Safety Certificate?

A gas safety check confirms your gas appliances are safe to use. A boiler service goes further: the engineer inspects, cleans and adjusts the boiler’s internal components so it runs efficiently and reliably.

Can I book a boiler service with my Gas Safety Certificate?

Yes. Booking both in the same visit saves time and costs less than booking them separately.

What’s checked during a boiler service?

The engineer follows the manufacturer’s instructions, typically checking combustion and flue gas readings, the flue, seals, burner and heat exchanger, controls, system pressure and safety devices. The service is recorded in the boiler’s Benchmark service record.

How long does a boiler service take?

Usually around an hour, depending on the boiler and its condition.

Who can service my boiler?

Only a Gas Safe registered engineer can legally work on gas boilers, including servicing.
