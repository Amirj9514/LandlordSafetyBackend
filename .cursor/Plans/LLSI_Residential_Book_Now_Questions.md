# LLSI Residential Book Now: Questions and Pricing (Final)

Source: LLSI Residential Price List (updated file, 23 Oct 2026).

## How pricing works
- Each card shows **"Starting from"**: the lowest price across all zones for that service.
- The exact price is shown after the customer enters their postcode. The postcode sets the zone (Central, Inner, Outer or M25).
- All prices are final, with **no VAT**.
- Congestion charge (£18) and parking are separate line items, as set out in the Terms & Conditions.
- Priority Service (+£99) is an optional extra, not a question on the card.

## 1. Gas Safety Certificate (CP12)
**Included:** meter and 1 appliance.

- **Q1: Number of appliances?**
  - Stepper on the right side, as on the site.
  - Info icon: count boilers, hobs/cookers and fires as appliances.
  - Each additional appliance: +£10.
- **Q2: CO alarm present at property?** Yes / No
  - Info icon: "A CO alarm is mandatory where a boiler is installed."
  - If **No**: offer "Fit a CO alarm for £35" (£40 in Zone 1).
- **Add-on:** Boiler Service (see section 2).

## 2. Boiler Service
**Starting from:** £79.99

**Standalone.** Customer picks one tier:
- Essential: £79.99
- Standard: £139.99
- Premium: £239.99

**Inside the CP12 add-on (bundle).** Customer picks one:
- CP12 + Essential: **£109.99** (2 appliances included). Fixed bundle price.

- CP12 + Premium: **£280.23** (5% bundle discount applied).

Only one bundle applies. No stacking.
